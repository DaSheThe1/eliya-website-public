# One-shot CTA emphasis contract

Select one approved conversion panel during intake. This module does not grant
permission to animate prices, scarcity, urgency, discounts, or every CTA.

- The base/server/CSS-failure state is the complete finished composition with
  its action visible. Animation may decorate that state; it may never reveal
  essential copy or the only button.
- Start only after a real scroll approach reaches the reviewed visibility
  threshold. A deep link, restored position, programmatic jump, missing
  observer, or stalled frame clock shows the finished static design instead of
  hiding and replaying content already on screen.
- Re-arm only after a full viewport exit and only after the prior sequence has
  finished. Boundary nudges must not restart or stutter it.
- If an armed state can hide any element, count painted frames while it is
  visibly armed and snap to the finished static state after the approved small
  budget. Use frames rather than a wall-clock race with IntersectionObserver.
- When the site motion opt-out is active, create no observer and no replay
  state. Show the final composition immediately.
- Every keyframe runs once. No `infinite`, pulse, throb, periodic sheen,
  countdown, fake capacity, or timed reactivation while the panel remains in
  view. A replay may require a deliberate full scroll away and back.

Freeze the actual visual order, duration, threshold, easing, copy, and CTA
authority per project. Pnina's value/free-call language and pricing are not
part of this reusable contract.
